# 💡 Business Prompt - Construction Project Dashboard

## 🎯 **USER PROMPT (Natural Business Question)**

> **"Create an executive project monitoring dashboard for construction management that shows project status, budget utilization, resource allocation, and performance tracking across our portfolio of ongoing construction projects. I need to see which projects are on track, over budget, and how our teams are performing."**

## 📋 **BUSINESS CONTEXT**

### **Stakeholder**: Construction Company Executive/Project Director
### **Use Case**: Portfolio Management & Executive Reporting
### **Decision Points**:
- Which projects need immediate attention?
- Are we staying within budget across the portfolio?
- How are our resources allocated?
- What's the completion status of each project?

## 🏢 **SCENARIO DETAILS**

### **Company Profile**:
- **Industry**: Construction & Engineering
- **Portfolio Size**: 30+ active projects
- **Project Types**: Engineering & Non-Residential
- **Budget Range**: $100K - $1M+ per project

### **Key Metrics Needed**:
1. **Project Completion %** - How complete are we?
2. **Budget Utilization** - Are we over/under budget?
3. **Resource Allocation** - Actual vs Planned resources
4. **Timeline Performance** - Days used vs allocated
5. **Work Status Distribution** - Not Started, In Progress, Completed
6. **Project Stage Analysis** - Plan, Design, Pre-construct phases

### **Executive Questions to Answer**:
- "How many projects are behind schedule?"
- "Which project managers are performing best?"
- "What's our overall budget variance trend?"
- "Where should I focus my attention this week?"

## 📊 **DATA STORY FRAMEWORK**

The dashboard will tell the story of:

1. **OVERVIEW**: Portfolio health at a glance (gauges, KPIs)
2. **STATUS**: Current project distribution (donuts, pie charts)
3. **PERFORMANCE**: Budget variance and resource tracking (combo charts, bars)
4. **DEEP DIVE**: Project-specific workload and timeline analysis

This creates a natural **narrative flow** from high-level executive summary down to actionable project details - exactly matching our reference dashboard structure.
